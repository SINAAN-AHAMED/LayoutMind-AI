# Original project artwork

The furniture and animals are original editable Three.js geometry. Their warm materials, soft silhouettes and readable details draw broad stylistic inspiration from life-simulation games; no models, textures or characters were extracted from those games.

The minimal architectural schematic is authored SVG at `frontend/public/images/architectural-blueprint.svg`. It has no visible text and uses the existing ivory/forest/brass palette.

## Aira

- Runtime artwork: `frontend/public/images/aira/view-0.webp` through `view-7.webp`: eight independently generated transparent RGBA illustrations, based on the earlier original concept. The generated art is stored locally as lossless WebP; decoded visible RGB pixels and all alpha values match the generated PNGs. No runtime image service is used.
- Renderer: `frontend/src/three/GuestSprite.tsx`; two-triangle quads select individual local images and turn horizontally toward the camera while staying upright. `GuestScene.tsx` retains floor-constrained movement, height and saves. Automatic views use camera azimuth with boundary hysteresis; the Look selector saves a preferred illustration. These are eight discrete illustrated poses, not a volumetric model or a continuous turntable. No old character mesh or model thumbnail is bundled.
- Panel: the welcome illustration, from the same artwork used in the room.
- Original concept: `docs/aira-concept.png`, the earlier 1024×1536 RGBA image; character identity, outfit and palette are retained.
- Character: original adult woman, age 25. No game model or franchise character was imported.

Eight-image generation: one isolated full-body adult Aira per transparent portrait image. Front welcome, slight left, left three-quarter, left over-shoulder, book held close, right over-shoulder, right hair sweep, and slight right variations; all look at the viewer. Preserve the original identity, teal eyes, blue-black hair, forest jacket, ivory blouse, charcoal skirt, gold accessories, book and boots. Request generous transparent margins, complete shoes, clean silhouettes, no detached fragments, scenery, text, borders or shadows. Pixel bounds are recorded in `frontend/src/lib/airaViews.json` to keep each figure grounded and equally tall with lossless encoding of the generated artwork. An early atlas experiment was rejected during visual QA for neighbouring-image bleed and is not included.

## Eva outfit

Eight original adult Aira variants are in `frontend/public/images/aira-eva/view-0.webp` through `view-7.webp`, with bounds in `frontend/src/lib/airaEvaViews.json`. Created with the built-in image-generation tool from the original Aira identity. The final hair revision uses compact shoulder-length hair tucked behind her shoulders, without the earlier long hair fan. The classic set retains its original hair and outfit.

Prompt set: preserve adult Aira’s refined face, teal eyes and gold hairpin; replace her classic outfit with a fully covered crimson, ivory, charcoal and teal EVA-inspired pilot suit, red gloves and integrated boots. Keep polished 2D painted anime style, full-body framing and genuine alpha transparency. Revise only the hair to compact shoulder length, then produce the welcome, slight-left, left three-quarter, left over-shoulder, clasped-hands, right over-shoulder, hair-touch and slight-right variations from that design. All face the viewer. No existing franchise character, insignia or model is imported. Long-haired suit experiments are not included.

The architectural SVG is authored directly for geometric accuracy. It represents an 8×8 m single-bedroom apartment at a consistent drawing scale, with a central entrance foyer and hall, a wide living-room opening, and separate kitchen, bedroom and bathroom doors. The existing local interior photographs are reused on the homepage; no external image URLs were introduced.

The door designs in `frontend/src/three/DoorModel.tsx` are original Three.js geometry: solid, panelled, fluted, privacy/clear glazing, steel grids, paired leaves and louvres. No external models or textures are required. The blueprint’s foyer route leads to the living room; the kitchen is a separate northern room.
